Support

Neptune Mobile, Neptune Vega, Neptune Mobile for Telco and Neptune Vega for Telco are demonstrations with simulated data. Nothing in them moves real money or touches a real account.

Something not working?

Close and reopen the app to reset the demo. The camera step in onboarding is optional: choose “Use the demo camera” or “Upload a photo instead”.
