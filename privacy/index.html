Privacy policy

Neptune Mobile, Neptune Vega, Neptune Mobile for Telco, Neptune Vega for Telco. Updated 28 October 2026.

These apps are demonstrations published by Neptune International for Software Technical Solutions, IT and Electronic Services LLC (“Neptune”). They show how our banking, wallet and telecom products work. Every account, balance, transaction, operator and customer in them is invented.

What we collect

Nothing. The apps have no sign-up with a real identity, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking. We do not receive, store or share any personal data from your use of them.

What stays on your device

Anything you type or capture while trying a demo stays on your device. In the onboarding demo, the camera is used to show an identity check. The pictures are processed on the device, are not uploaded and are discarded when you leave the flow. The on-device checks are for demonstration and are not a certified biometric or liveness system.

Permissions

The camera permission is requested only when you choose to try the identity-check demo. You can refuse it and use the simulated camera or upload a picture instead.

Children

The apps contain no real services and collect nothing, so they pose no data risk to children.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes we will publish the new version at this address. Questions: info@neptune.ly · neptune.ly/contact.
