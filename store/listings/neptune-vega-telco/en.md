## Description (max 4000)
Neptune Vega for Telco (showroom edition) demonstrates a wallet-first mobile-money service for telecom operators, with identity onboarding inside the app and an agent network. All data is invented.

• On-device onboarding: passport, live selfie and face match (demo-grade)
• Agents map and cash in or out with one-time codes
• Demo Agent mode to see the other side of a cash-in
• Send, receive, pay and savings pots
• SIM or eSIM activation after account creation
• English, French and Arabic, six looks, light and dark

Demonstration only. This app uses simulated data. No real accounts, money, identities or institutions are involved, and nothing you enter leaves your device. On-device identity checks are demo-grade and are not a certified biometric or liveness system.
