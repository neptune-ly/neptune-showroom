## Description (max 4000)
Neptune Vega (showroom edition) is an interactive demonstration of the Neptune white-label wallet platform for banks, telecom operators and fintechs, with Personal and Business modes.

• Personal wallet: send, request, QR pay and cash in or out at agents
• Business mode: orders, payment links, settlement and refunds
• Mizan tap-to-pay point of sale (simulated): amount, tap, plan choice, approval
• Pay over time with Mizan in Personal mode
• KYC upgrade with on-device identity checks
• Six switchable looks, light and dark, English, French and Arabic

Demonstration only. This app uses simulated data. No real accounts, money, identities or institutions are involved, and nothing you enter leaves your device. On-device identity checks are demo-grade and are not a certified biometric or liveness system.
