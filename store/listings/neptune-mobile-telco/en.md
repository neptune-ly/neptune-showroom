## Description (max 4000)
Neptune Mobile for Telco (showroom edition) shows how a telecom operator can offer a mobile wallet with full digital onboarding inside the app. All operators, numbers and balances are invented.

• On-device onboarding: passport capture, text-zone reading, live selfie and face match (demo-grade)
• SIM or eSIM activation animation after account creation
• Wallet, send, top-up, data bundles and bills
• Scan and My QR, virtual card and rewards
• Operator-style look with five more looks to compare
• English, French and Arabic, light and dark

Demonstration only. This app uses simulated data. No real accounts, money, identities or institutions are involved, and nothing you enter leaves your device. On-device identity checks are demo-grade and are not a certified biometric or liveness system.
