## Description (max 4000)
Neptune Mobile (showroom edition) is an interactive demonstration of the Neptune white-label retail banking app, built for banks, fintechs and partners evaluating Neptune.

• Accounts and balances with a clean, calm home screen
• Transfers with a dedicated amount keypad and one-time-code confirmation
• LYPay and OnePay style instant payments, QR scan and My QR
• Card management: freeze, limits, PIN, virtual cards
• Pay over time with Mizan: choose a plan, see everything disclosed, confirm and track instalments
• Switch between professional and vibrant looks, light and dark, English, French and Arabic

Demonstration only. This app uses simulated data. No real accounts, money, identities or institutions are involved, and nothing you enter leaves your device. On-device identity checks are demo-grade and are not a certified biometric or liveness system.
